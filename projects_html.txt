          <div class="proj-title">2DOF Robotic Manipulator</div>
          <p class="proj-body">A complete kinematic mapping and trajectory planning model built in MATLAB Simulink. The project involved exporting a Simscape Multibody Rigid Body Tree, establishing Forward Kinematics for pose estimation, and utilizing the Levenberg-Marquardt solver for Inverse Kinematics. This enables the end-effector to trace accurate geometric paths with governed acceleration.</p>

          <div class="proj-num">Simulation</div>
          <div class="proj-title">CFD Analysis of IC Engine Piston Heads</div>
          <p class="proj-body">An analytical project conducting reactive-flow CFD simulations in ANSYS Forte to evaluate CI engine combustion. By comparing Flat-top and Circular Dish geometries, we utilized the RANS RNG k-epsilon turbulence model and CHEMKIN-Pro solver. The dish geometry demonstrated superior squish-driven air-fuel mixing, resulting in near-complete combustion.</p>
